DJ / PRODUCER / UK

Raised on the harder edge of British dance music.

Freddie Fierce draws from the sound of late-90s and early-2000s hard house — dark rooms, rolling basslines, hoovers and records built for stupid hours of the morning.

Digging through the past without trying to recreate it, his productions bring that sound into something new.
